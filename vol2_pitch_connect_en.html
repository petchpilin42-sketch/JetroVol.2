OUTLINE

Event Background & Mission

This seminar and business matching event is the second installment of a three-part series connecting industrial manufacturers in Thailand with leading Japanese DX and AI technology providers.
While Vol. 1 established initial touchpoints around organizational transformation, talent, and AI decision-making, Vol. 2 focuses on creating direct business collaborations through high-density pitch presentations and open roundtable discussions between Thai factory operators and Japanese solution innovators.

3 Key Value Highlights

Accelerating validated commercial matching between factory operations and tech providers

01 PITCH

Rapid Assessment of Japanese DX & AI Solutions

Approximately 10 vetted Japanese solution providers present practical industrial applications for Thai factories, enabling fast, side-by-side evaluation of high-ROI technologies.

02 CONSULTATION

Direct Consultations on Specific Bottlenecks

Following the pitch session, dedicated breakout tables facilitate 1-on-1 dialogue. Address urgent operational challenges directly—including labor shortages, quality inspection, and energy savings.

03 BUSINESS MATCH

Stepping Stone to Full Commercial Negotiations

Far more than passive listening, this event creates validated touchpoints that feed directly into structured bilateral matchmaking and proof-of-concept (PoC) initiatives scheduled for November.
